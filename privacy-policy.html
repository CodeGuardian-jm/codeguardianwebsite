Privacy Policy

LAST UPDATED: MAY 2026

Overview

Code Guardian operates as a business automation service provider based in Jamaica. This policy explains how we handle data when our system interacts with business communications on behalf of our clients.

Data We Collect

Messages sent to connected WhatsApp and Instagram business accounts, sender identifiers, timestamps, and transaction data entered into the system by the business owner.

How We Use Data

To route messages to the appropriate business owner, to log interactions for business reporting, and to automate responses on behalf of the business client.

Data Storage

All data is stored securely in Google Sheets accessible only to the authorized business owner. We do not sell or share data with third parties under any circumstances.

Third Party Services

We integrate with Meta APIs (WhatsApp and Instagram), Google Apps Script, and Discord for message routing and automation purposes.

User Rights

Any business or individual whose data passes through our system has the right to request deletion of their data at any time by contacting us directly.

Contact

For privacy concerns contact: admin@codeguardian.site
